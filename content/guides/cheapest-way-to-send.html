The two costs in every transfer

  1. The fee, shown upfront, for example A$5.
  2. The exchange rate margin, the gap between the provider's rate and the real mid-market rate you see on Google. This is often bigger than the fee, and it's easy to miss.

A quick example

Provider AProvider B
FeeA$0A$6
Rate (mid-market 88.00)86.5088.00
Family receives on A$1,000NPR 86,500NPR 87,472

The "free" option delivers almost NPR 1,000 less. Always compare the final rupee amount.

Ways to pay less

  • Use first-transfer promotions, but compare again on later transfers.
  • Pay by bank transfer or PayID instead of a credit card (cards can add cash-advance fees).
  • Pick economy speed if it's not urgent.
  • Send fewer, larger transfers when there is a flat fee.
