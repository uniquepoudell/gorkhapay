## Post 1: scam warning (suits: all groups, especially workers and families)

⚠️ "Best rate to Nepal, inbox me" 🚩

Before you send money through someone on Facebook, check one thing: are they on the AUSTRAC Remittance Sector Register? Every business that sends money overseas from Australia must be registered, and anyone can search the register for free by business name or ABN.

Red flags:
• Won't show the rate or fee in writing
• Asks you to pay into a personal bank account
• No receipt, no tracking number
• "Rate valid for 10 minutes only"

फेसबुकमा "राम्रो रेट" भन्दै आउने मान्छेलाई पैसा दिनुअघि AUSTRAC मा दर्ता छ कि छैन जाँच गर्नुहोस्। हुन्डी गैरकानुनी हो, पैसा हरायो भने फिर्ता पाइँदैन।

## Post 2: protect parents in Nepal (suits: families, students)

Quick reminder for everyone sending money home 🙏

Tell your family in Nepal: never share the transfer PIN or MTCN with anyone who calls them, even if the caller says they're from the bank or the remittance company. Only you and the receiver need that number.

घरमा बुबाआमालाई भनिदिनुहोस्: पैसा निकाल्ने PIN/MTCN नम्बर कसैलाई पनि फोनमा नदिनुहोस्, बैंक वा रेमिट कम्पनीबाट हो भने पनि।

If something goes wrong, call your bank in Australia straight away and report it to Scamwatch. More tips here:
https://uniquepoudell.github.io/gorkhapay/guides/spot-remittance-scam.html

## Post 3: compare before you send (suits: students, workers)

Sending money to Nepal this month? 💸

A "zero fee" transfer isn't always the cheapest. The real cost is often in the exchange rate. Compare how many rupees your family actually receives, then pick a licensed provider.
