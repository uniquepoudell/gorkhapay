and the About page already discloses referral fees.

## Later and adjacent income
- **New-to-Australia content** (OSHC student health cover, SIM plans, opening a bank account, TFN) could earn through
  Commission Factory advertisers. Health insurance is a financial product: keep it factual, compare features only,
  no "best for you" recommendations without a licence (ASIC finfluencer guidance).
- **Direct sponsors**: Nepali-run businesses in Australia (remittance agents, migration agents registered with MARA,
  accountants, restaurants, grocery stores) for the featured-agent spot. Only licensed remittance businesses and
  MARA-registered agents may be featured. The owner closes any deal; runs may draft a one-page sponsor pack.
- **AdSense** after a custom domain (gorkhapay.com.np) and more guides.
- **Crypto exchanges (e.g. Swyftx pays 30% of trading fees, AUD, needs ABN details)**: do NOT add to either site.
  Crypto trading is illegal for people in Nepal, News Nepal's audience is partly in Nepal, and crypto promotion
  carries ASIC risk. Listed here only so runs don't re-propose it.

## Rules that affect earning
- ASIC: factual information about financial products is fine; personal recommendations need an AFS licence.
  Always disclose paid links. Never write "the best provider for you".
- Remitly and Wise terms likely forbid bidding on their brand names in ads and misleading claims; runs must
  never state their fees or rates without a source checked that day.
